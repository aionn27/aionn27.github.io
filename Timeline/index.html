ARCHE: A Timeline of Philosophy for Beginners

Philosophy, by its major periods:

Philosophy is usually divided into four major periods. Each period has a distinct focus depending on what is going on with culture and society at that time, or what ideas are most enticing to engage in or respond to. I personally find the Modern Period to be the most enjoyable to learn about, because a lot of figures, ideas, and works engage in or overlap with modern science. Rest assured no matter what period you learn about, one thing is certain: They're all nerds.
